How scores, sources and priorities work

These are transparent role-weighted comparisons, not a DPS simulator or a best-in-slot list. Item level does not decide the score. Each recommendation explains its stat gains and losses. Weights do not model talents, stat caps, combat encounters or full weapon-pair optimisation.

Two-handed replacements include the off-hand being lost. Illegal off-hand swaps and unique duplicates are rejected. Procs, use effects, set bonuses, scaling and unresolved random rolls are marked for review. A promising partial score is not a guaranteed upgrade.

Future items are limited to five levels above each character. Quest rewards must pass class, race, prerequisite-path and quest-giver/turn-in faction checks. Unverified class/faction paths are omitted. A quest not in the saved log still needs an availability check. Hostile vendors and vendors with unknown faction data are excluded for each character; neutral merchants remain available. Vendor reputation, stock, extended costs, guild permissions and crafting workstations can also need verification. Recipe materials are checked per craft and are not reserved across recommendations.

Items owned by opposite-faction characters are excluded, including account-bound items. Group plans count one best quest reward per character and each shared owned item once. Effort bands favour owned items, ready hand-ins, learned recipes with materials and affordable simple purchases; they are estimates, not travel times. Individual alternatives may compete for one item; they are not a simultaneous shopping list. Base reputation and spell/script conditions are not a complete eligibility simulation.
