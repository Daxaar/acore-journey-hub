Transactions

Recorded loot, vendor trades and Auction House activity for your characters.

Auction entries are server observations made before database commit. Settlement amounts include the returned deposit. They represent mail prepared, rather than money collected. Removals alone do not establish a cancellation. Existing auctions may have no recorded listing. Vendor amounts cover copper; token costs, refunds and buyback purchases are outside this version.
